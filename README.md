# Statistical-Analysis-using-Python
This project analyzes survey data, exploring variations in technology stacks and experience levels across different roles and geographic regions. It includes an examination of the most in-demand technologies by region, the popularity of various roles, working preferences, and interest in emerging technologies.

Over the past three years, Full Stack Developer roles have been the most sought after. Amazon Web Services (AWS) topped the list of emerging platforms in 2023 and 2024, followed by Microsoft Azure. The top five most popular web frameworks in 2023 include "Spring Boot," "Node.js," "jQuery," "Angular," and "Flask."

Blockchain technology has had mixed feedback over the last two years. Of the 3,215 respondents, many were indifferent, with 2,445 expressing a "very unfavorable" view and just 1,236 selecting "very favorable." Tools like "npm" and "Docker" have seen high popularity in both India and the USA over the last two years.

Regarding working environments, preferences have shifted towards hybrid and remote settings, with the number of hybrid workers growing from 4,882 in 2022 to 15,844 in 2023. In-person work environments remain the least common.

In 2024, the most in-demand skills included the .NET Framework, React Native, and the Spring Framework among various other technologies.
